# Frontend

The website, made with the help of Claude (AI). The AI written parts are marked in each file.

- **index.html** - the page itself; the intro, then the 4 steps (choose stocks, hedge, optimise, backtest). A lot of this was written by me.
- **Static/JS/app.js** - makes the page work; sends everything to app.py and draws the backtest chart with Chart.js. Entirely AI written.
- **Static/CSS/style.css** - the styling. Colours and fonts are mine, the layout and buttons were AI.
- **Static/Images** - the logo and the efficient frontier picture.
