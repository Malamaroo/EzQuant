Welcome to EzQuant, your gateway to quantitative finance education!

EzQuant helps you learn the principles of quantitative finance through a fun, digestible journey. You will learn the fundamentals of portfolio management, hedging, and optimisation; based upon Harry' Markowitz's Modern Portfolio Theory. In other words, you will learn how to create a portfolio that both balances risk, and then maximises returns for your specified risk. Therefore, you will:

1. Choose your stocks

2. Hedge your portfolio (optional and repeatable)

3. Optimise your portfolio

4. Backtest your portfolio, and compare performance with other trading strategies!

5. Repeat steps 1-4 until you are happy with your portfolio

Step 1: Choose your stocks

You have a total allowance of $1,000,000

Each stock must be purchased with a minimum of $5,000

Please enter your desired value for each stock, and then tick the corresponding checkbox.

Please press Save Portfolio to check your current portfolio or commit your portfolio. Furthermore, please press Reset Portfolio to start over.

Step 2 (optional): Hedge your portfolio

Hedging suggests a stock that has the most negatively correlated variance compared to your current portfolio. Traders add hedges to reduce a portfolio's risk. If you like, you can add this recommended stock to your portfolio, thereby reducing its potential risk. Hedging is both optional and repeatable.

Step 3: Optimise your portfolio

The following optimisation is based on Mean-variance optimisation. The mathematical framework is derived from Harry Markowitz's Modern Portfolio Theory. The notion is that the formula will optimise your portfolio to deliver the highest possible expected return for a given level of risk , the lowest risk for a given target return, or the lowest possible risk. In this instance, the optimisation will be based upon maximising returns for a given level of risk. So, please specify what level of risk you are comfortable with. This is measured as the volatility of the portfolio. Effectively, your portfolio will sit on the efficient frontier for your specified volatility.

Step 4: Backtest your portfolio

Backtesting is the process of testing a trading strategy on historical data to see how it would have performed. It allows traders to evaluate the effectiveness of their strategies and make informed decisions about their trading approach. By simulating trades based on past market conditions, backtesting helps traders identify potential strengths and weaknesses in their strategies, enabling them to refine and optimize their approach before risking real capital in live markets.
